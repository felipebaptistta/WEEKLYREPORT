Quick Read

Corn inspections took a breather this week, dropping to 1.22 MMT from the robust 1.66 MMT seen previously, though cumulative shipments of 44.1 MMT still maintain a massive lead over last year’s pace. Soybean inspections followed suit, cooling to 685k MT as the seasonal "pivot" toward Brazil's record harvest becomes more pronounced in the shipping channels. Wheat volume remained sluggish at 315k MT, tracking slightly lower on the week and falling behind the same period last year.



Overall, while the weekly volumes saw a significant "step down" across the board, the year-to-date totals for corn and wheat remain the primary pillars of support for the U.S. export program.
