Key Observations

February soybean crush totaled 208.785 million bushels — well above trade expectations (\~202.7 mbu average) and a new record for any February.

This marks the strongest February crush on record and confirms continued strong U.S. processing demand despite the seasonal slowdown from January’s 221.56 mbu.

Soybean oil stocks jumped to 2.080 billion pounds (13-year high territory), rising 9.5% month-over-month and +38.4% YoY. This reflects robust oil production from the elevated crush pace and builds a comfortable supply buffer heading into spring.

Relationship with USDA WASDE

The USDA March WASDE already incorporated stronger processing demand (raising the 2025/26 U.S. soybean crush forecast by 5 million bushels). Current NOPA run-rate is now +7–8% above last year, suggesting the full-year crush could comfortably exceed USDA’s projection and support even tighter meal/oil balances later in 2026.
