argentina - SOYBEAN

During the last few days, rainfall continued across a large part of the agricultural area; as a result, at the national level

for soybean, the Optimum/Favourable water condition has increased by 7 percentage points compared to the previous

report, with the central and southeastern areas of Buenos Aires showing a significant improvement following the water

deficit recorded during much of the season. Likewise, the Normal/Excellent crop condition has also recorded a weekon-week increase of 2 percentage points, while first-crop soybean, which is close to harvest, shows expected yields

averaging 3.5 tons per hectare in the Northern Core and 3.7 per hectare in the Southern Core according to field reports.

Meanwhile, 74.7% of second-crop soybean is currently going through its critical period, with 67% of the planted area

under a Normal/Excellent crop condition, showing a slight improvement after the latest rainfall; under this scenario, we

maintain our production forecast at 48.5 million tons.

CORN

Regarding corn, harvest is progressing nationwide and has reached 13% of the suitable area, with fieldwork mainly

concentrated in the Northern Core, where progress maintains a good pace and yields average 9.8 tons per hectare. At

the same time, the Southern Core is beginning to gain momentum in harvesting activities, with yields around 8.6 tons

per hectare. As for late-planted corn, most of the agricultural area is going through grain filling stages, with 85.2% of

the area under Adequate to Optimal water conditions, following a week-on-week increase of 4.4 percentage points,

and 90% of the crop under Normal to Excellent condition. Under this scenario, we maintain our production forecast at

57 million tons.
