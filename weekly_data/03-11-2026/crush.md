\## Key Observations



January soybean crush totaled \*\*221.56 million bushels\*\*, representing the strongest January on record and confirming continued expansion in U.S. soybean processing capacity.



While crush declined slightly from the record \*\*224.99 mbu\*\* reached in December, the year-over-year increase of more than \*\*10%\*\* highlights strong demand for soybean meal and vegetable oils.



Soybean oil stocks rose sharply to \*\*1.90 billion pounds\*\*, exceeding market expectations and reflecting strong oil production following the elevated crush pace.



\## Relationship with USDA WASDE



The USDA March WASDE increased the \*\*U.S. soybean crush forecast by 5 million bushels\*\*, citing stronger processing demand supported by recent NOPA data.



Current NOPA crush levels are running \*\*+7.2% above last year\*\*, suggesting processing activity may continue to exceed USDA's full-year growth projection of approximately \*\*+5%\*\*.
