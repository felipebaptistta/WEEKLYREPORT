# Export Inspections & Sales — Week of 2026-03-11

## USDA Weekly Export Inspections (week ending 2026-03-05)

### Corn
- Weekly inspections:     1.214 MMT   (prior week: 1.089 MMT; prior year: 1.342 MMT)
- Cumulative YTD:         22.4 MMT
- USDA annual target:     63.5 MMT    (35% of target shipped with ~28 weeks remaining)
- Top destinations:       Mexico (0.31 MMT), Japan (0.22 MMT), South Korea (0.18 MMT)

Inspections rebounded from a soft prior week but remain below the year-ago pace needed
to meet the annual target. Mexico remains the dominant buyer of U.S. corn.

### Soybeans
- Weekly inspections:     0.842 MMT   (prior week: 0.914 MMT; prior year: 0.773 MMT)
- Cumulative YTD:         38.7 MMT
- USDA annual target:     52.2 MMT    (74% of target shipped — ahead of pace)
- Top destinations:       China (0.41 MMT), Unknown (0.19 MMT), Netherlands (0.09 MMT)

Soybean inspections remain solid relative to last year. China continued buying at a
measured pace despite Brazilian competition. The YTD pace is comfortable vs. the USDA
target but expected to slow materially as Brazilian harvest accelerates.

### Wheat
- Weekly inspections:     0.378 MMT   (prior week: 0.291 MMT; prior year: 0.412 MMT)
- Cumulative YTD:         17.6 MMT
- USDA annual target:     23.4 MMT    (75% of target shipped — on pace)
- Top destinations:       Philippines (0.11 MMT), Mexico (0.09 MMT), Nigeria (0.07 MMT)

Wheat inspections improved from the prior week but remain below year-ago. Black Sea
origins continue to compete aggressively, limiting U.S. market share in key destinations.
