# Weather & Crop Conditions — Week of 2026-03-11

## United States

### Winter Wheat
- Crop condition (as of 2026-03-08):  44% good/excellent  (vs. 46% last week, vs. 55% last year)
- Poor/very poor:  21%  (vs. 18% last week)
- Key concern: persistent dryness across the southern Plains (Kansas, Oklahoma, Texas panhandle).
  The 7-day forecast shows below-normal precipitation and above-normal temperatures for the
  hard red winter wheat belt. Topsoil moisture rated 41% short-to-very-short in Kansas.
- Northern Plains: adequate moisture; soft red winter wheat in the Ohio/Indiana corridor rated
  58% good/excellent — well above the national average.

### Spring Planting Outlook
- Too early for corn and soybean planting in most of the Corn Belt.
- 6–14 day outlook: near-normal temperatures for Iowa, Illinois, Indiana; no significant
  precipitation threat to delay fieldwork when the season opens.

## South America

### Brazil (Mato Grosso, Paraná, Rio Grande do Sul)
- Soybean harvest: 71% complete as of 2026-03-07 (vs. 65% last week; vs. 58% same week last year)
- Harvest pace ahead of average; pod-fill conditions were favorable.
- Estimated production: 127.8 MMT (CONAB March, in line with USDA's 127 MMT).
- Second-crop corn (safrinha): planting 96% complete; crop rated mostly in good condition.
  Key risk: late-planted safrinha in Mato Grosso faces a tighter yield window if dry
  conditions emerge in April during grain-fill.

### Argentina
- Corn: 18% harvested; crop rated 41% good/excellent, 27% poor/very poor.
  Buenos Aires province recovering from February dryness after recent rains (20–40mm),
  but yield expectations already reduced. BCBA estimate: 49.5 MMT (down from 51 MMT).
- Soybeans: pod-fill in progress; recent rainfall beneficial but came late for early-planted fields.
  BCBA estimate: 48.2 MMT.
