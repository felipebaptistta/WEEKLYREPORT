## Overall WASDE Tone
Broadly bearish for all three commodities. U.S. corn stocks were in line with expectations
but world stocks were higher than anticipated. Soybean and wheat both showed higher-than-
expected ending stocks. The March WASDE reinforced the well-supplied global narrative
that has pressured prices throughout Q1 2026.
